ขอข้อมูล
01

Turbine Actuation

Governors, signal converters, and electro-hydraulic actuators that translate a control command into precise valve movement.

Mechanical Governors & Actuators

Proven mechanical-hydraulic governors and actuators for speed control on steam and gas turbines and reciprocating engines.

CPCII Current-to-Pressure Converter

Converts a 4–20 mA control signal into a proportional hydraulic pressure to drive turbine actuators.

TM25 Hydro-Mechanical Actuator

Compact hydraulic actuator delivering accurate valve positioning for small to mid-size steam turbines.

VariStroke Actuator (Integrated)

Electro-hydraulic linear actuator with an integrated servo for steam-turbine valve control and easy retrofit.

VariStroke Actuator (Remote Servo)

Linear electro-hydraulic actuator with a remote servo for flexible installation on governor valves.

VariStroke Servo

High-response servo unit driving the VariStroke family for accurate, repeatable valve stroke.

02

Speed Sensing

Reliable rotational-speed feedback for governing and overspeed protection.

MPU Magnetic Pickups

Passive magnetic speed sensors providing dependable speed feedback to governors and protection systems.

03

Safety Systems

Certified overspeed detection and mechanical trip devices that bring the turbine to a safe state.

ProTech GII / TPS Total Protection System

Triple-redundant (TMR) overspeed and process protection with SIL-certified trip logic for turbines.

ProTech-SX Protection System

Compact simplex overspeed protection delivering fast, certified trip response.

QuickTrip Trip Block Assembly

Hydraulic trip block that rapidly dumps actuator pressure to close valves on a trip signal.

04

Customized Turbine Controls

High-availability, fully programmable platforms for critical turbine and compressor machinery.

Flex500

Integrated turbine control and operator interface for steam-turbine generator and mechanical-drive packages.

MicroNet Plus Dual Redundant Control

Programmable dual-redundant control platform for critical turbine and compressor applications.

GAP Graphical Application Programmer

Graphical programming environment for configuring MicroNet and Flex control applications.

MicroNet TMR Fault-Tolerant Control

Triple-modular-redundant control system for the highest availability on critical machinery.

05

Standard Configurable Controls

Off-the-shelf, configurable digital controls for steam-turbine generator and mechanical-drive service.

505D / 505XT Turbine Control

Configurable digital control for single- and split-range steam turbines with an integrated operator display.

Peak200 Mechanical Drive Speed Control

Cost-effective speed control for mechanical-drive steam turbines.

505DR Dual Redundant Control

Dual-redundant version of the 505 for higher-availability steam-turbine control.

2301E-ST

Load-sharing and speed control for steam turbines in generator and mechanical-drive service.

5009XT Fault-Tolerant Control

Triple-redundant control for multi-valve steam turbines requiring maximum uptime.
